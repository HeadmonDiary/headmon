Built from lived experience

Less effort when the day is already difficult.

Headmon grew out of daily life with chronic headaches and migraine. It has been shaped from the beginning together with someone who lives with them, so reducing effort during a difficult episode—not simply adding more features—guides the design.

Log what matters. Leave the rest.

Start quickly, add context when you can, and let patterns build privately over time.

In the app

A diary, not a dashboard you have to manage.

The Today view keeps the current day close. Medicines stay recognizable, and Insights translate the history into approachable summaries rather than medical predictions.

Local-only by design

Your diary, photos and history are stored on your device. Headmon has no account system or diary server.

⇄

Portable when you choose

Readable JSON, CSV and PDF exports let you keep a backup or move diary data between Android and iOS.

◐

Made for real episodes

Quick entry, reminders, widgets, reports and low-stimulation tools reduce friction when a screen is the last thing you want.
